# Terms of Service Brainstorm

1. **User Eligibility**
   - Minimum age verification required for all users.
   - Comprehensive ID validation (KYC) required before any creator account can be activated.

2. **Content Policy**
   - Strict prohibition on illegal content, CSAM, non-consensual media, etc.
   - Creators are solely responsible for the content they upload and ensuring they hold all necessary rights.

3. **Payment Terms**
   - Platform operates exclusively on USDC on the Base network.
   - Non-custodial model: users control their own funds.
   - Platform commission: 12.5% default rate, 10% for Enclave tier.
   - All transactions are final; no refunds are provided due to the nature of blockchain transactions.

4. **Referral Program**
   - Referrers can earn a 1% revenue share of referred creators' earnings, OR
   - A one-time $50 / $75 bonus structure based on specific milestones.

5. **Data Retention**
   - Content is deleted immediately upon account closure.
   - Personal profile data is retained for 18 months post-closure.
   - (See Data Retention Policy for full details).

6. **Intellectual Property (IP)**
   - Creators retain full ownership of their content.
   - Creators grant PoDM a worldwide, non-exclusive license to host, display, and distribute the content on the platform.

7. **Liability**
   - PoDM is not responsible for the security of users' personal crypto wallets.
   - PoDM is not responsible for network gas fees or transaction failures on the Base network.
   - PoDM is not liable for fluctuations in the price or stability of USDC.

8. **Taxes**
   - Each creator is acting as an independent contractor.
   - Creators are solely responsible for calculating and remitting their own tax obligations.

9. **Termination**
   - PoDM reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts for Terms of Service violations, chargeback abuse, or illegal activity without prior notice.

10. **Crypto Risk**
    - Users acknowledge that blockchain transactions are permanent and irreversible.
    - USDC stability is maintained by Circle; PoDM does not guarantee the value or stability of USDC.

11. **Dispute Resolution**
    - Platform policy is strictly no refunds for digital goods.
    - All disputes between fans and creators must be handled via the internal support ticket system.
