# Data Retention Policy

This policy outlines how PoDM handles user data upon account closure and during normal operations.

## Account Closure
- **18-Month Retention Period**: Initiating an account closure triggers an 18-month retention period for profile data.
- **Content Deletion**: All user-uploaded content (images, videos, etc.) stored in R2 is removed immediately upon account closure.
- **Profile Data**: Profile data including email addresses, ID verification documents, and other PII is securely retained for the 18-month retention period before being permanently deleted.

## Transaction Records
- **Permanent Retention**: Transaction records are retained permanently for legal, accounting, and compliance purposes.
- **Blockchain Immutability**: All USDC on Base payments are processed via smart contracts on a public blockchain. These on-chain records are immutable and permanent by design, completely outside the control of PoDM.

## Data Export
- **User Rights**: Users have the right to request a complete export of their personal data prior to initiating account closure. Please contact support to initiate a data export request.
