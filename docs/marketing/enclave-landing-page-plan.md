### 6. FAQ Section
**Goal**: Address objections and questions

**Q: What is The Enclave?**
A: The Enclave is our founding creator program. The first 50 creators to join PoDM receive lifetime benefits including a 90/10 revenue split, priority support, and the ability to shape platform features.

**Q: How long does the 90/10 split last?**
A: Forever. As an Enclave member, you lock in the 90/10 split for life, even as the platform grows.

**Q: What content is allowed?**
A: All legal content is welcome - adult content, AI-generated content, artistic content, etc. We have clear, consistent policies with no surprise changes.

**Q: How do I get paid?**
A: Payments are processed through Stripe with weekly payouts directly to your bank account.

**Q: Can I stay on other platforms?**
A: Absolutely! Many creators use multiple platforms. We encourage you to diversify.

**Q: What happens after 50 creators join?**
A: The Enclave closes permanently. Future creators will have standard terms (85/15 split). This is a one-time opportunity.

**Q: Is my identity kept private?**
A: Yes. PoDM operates as a velvet rope platform. We never publicly disclose Enclave members or feature creators in marketing materials without explicit permission.

**Q: What if I don't have an existing audience?**
A: While we prioritize creators with established audiences for The Enclave, we evaluate each application individually. Show us your plan and potential.

## Technical Implementation

### Frontend Location
- Path: `podm-frontend/src/pages/Enclave.tsx`
- Route: `/enclave` or `/join-enclave`

### Components Needed
1. `EnclaveHero.tsx` - Hero section with counter
2. `EnclaveValueProps.tsx` - Three pillars section
3. `EnclaveComparison.tsx` - Comparison table
4. `EnclaveBenefits.tsx` - Detailed benefits
5. `EnclaveFAQ.tsx` - FAQ accordion
6. `EnclaveApplicationForm.tsx` - Application form
7. `SpotsCounter.tsx` - Live spots remaining counter

### Backend Requirements
1. **Application Submission Endpoint**
   - `POST /api/v1/enclave/applications`
   - Store in `enclave_applications` table
   - Send confirmation email
   - Notify admin team

2. **Spots Counter Endpoint**
   - `GET /api/v1/enclave/spots-remaining`
   - Returns count of accepted creators (max 50)

3. **Database Table**: `enclave_applications`
```sql
CREATE TABLE enclave_applications (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  full_name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
  email VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL UNIQUE,
  phone VARCHAR(50),
  current_platform VARCHAR(100),
  follower_count VARCHAR(50),
  monthly_earnings VARCHAR(50),
  content_type VARCHAR(100),
  why_join TEXT,
  how_heard VARCHAR(100),
  referral_code VARCHAR(50),
  status VARCHAR(50) DEFAULT 'pending', -- pending, accepted, rejected
  created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT NOW(),
  reviewed_at TIMESTAMP,
  reviewed_by UUID REFERENCES users(id)
);
```
